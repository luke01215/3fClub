Youth Club

Distinguished Expert is not a participation award

It means a score of at least 190 out of 200, at fifty feet, ten separate times. Each bullseye is worth ten points. That is a lot of bullseyes.

The Youth Club meets Mondays and Thursdays. Instruction is free, and members do the teaching.
