Conservation

The society exists to look after the ground it sits on

Members plant pine, wildflower and clover to build habitat. Ponds were dug and are maintained for nesting waterfowl. Birds are caught in mist nets, measured, banded and released for future study.

The people doing that work are the same people who fish and hunt here. That is not a coincidence — it is the whole idea.
