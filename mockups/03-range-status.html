The property

Facilities

All facilities →

Skeet & Trap

Five skeet fields, two trap fields. Winter league runs 20 weeks and ends in a banquet; the summer league runs 10.

7 FIELDS · LEAGUES YEAR-ROUND

Rifle & Pistol

Concrete firing line at 100 yards, second line at 200. Sign in at the gate, red light on and the flag hung before anyone shoots.

200 YD · KEY CARD ACCESS

Archery

Indoor range plus a 3-D course through pines and hardwoods, with stationary targets at 20, 30 and 40 yards.

INDOOR · 3-D COURSE

Ponds & Fields

Four ponds including a 32-acre stocked lake, plus hunting fields with a tree stand sign-in board at the gate.

4 PONDS · 402 ACRES
