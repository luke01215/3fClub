Conservation

Defend from waste our country's air, soil, minerals, forests, water and wildlife

Conservation is not a side program here — it is the reason the society exists. Members study birds and waterfowl through their spring and fall migration, catching them in mist nets, recording data, fitting leg bands, and releasing them for future study.

By planting pine, wildflower and clover, members contribute directly to the habitat. Ponds have been created and maintained for nesting waterfowl. The work is done by the people who hunt and fish this ground, which is precisely the point.

Our facility invites you to bring your entire family to enjoy a total outdoor experience.
