Disciplines

Seven ways to spend a Saturday

Every discipline here is run by a committee of members. Nobody is paid, the ranges are maintained by the people who shoot them, and instruction is free to anyone who asks.

Skeet & Trap

Five skeet fields, two trap fields. Winter league runs twenty weeks and finishes with a banquet; summer runs ten and mixes both.

Range Safety

The rules are short, and they are not negotiable

Every member signs off on the current range rules before their gate key card is activated. When the range committee issues a revision, everybody signs again.

That sounds strict. It is why this club has the record it does.

Read the Full Rules

Outdoor Range

Rev. 07/2025
  1. Sign in on entering. The book is in the mailbox on the storage container. Eye and ear protection required.
  2. Red light on, red flag up at the 100-yard line before anyone shoots.
  3. Muzzles downrange at all times. Benched guns: action open, chamber empty, magazine well empty.
  4. Nobody goes forward until a cease fire is called and acknowledged by every person on the range.
  5. Shooters 18 or older, or accompanied by an adult.
  6. Paper and cardboard targets only, on the provided stands. No steel, no spinners, no cans.
  7. Drills are fine. Indiscriminate rapid fire and magazine dumps are not.
  8. Guests $5, signed in, and they read these rules first.
The Season

The club year, start to finish

Membership is not a summer thing. There is a reason to be here in every month of the calendar.

Mar – May

Spring

Martin houses go back up. Songbird banding through the migration. Youth Club Mondays and Thursdays. 3-D archery opens.

Jun – Aug

Summer

The 3F Summer Classic. Purple martin banding before the birds leave for Brazil. Kids Fishing Derby. Applications open August 1.

Sep – Nov

Fall

Pheasant releases four days a week. Deer season, stands signed in and mapped. Youth Day. Fall gun raffle and deer processing.

Dec – Feb

Winter

Twenty weeks of skeet league, then the banquet. Cross-country skiing. Indoor archery and pistol. The Christmas party.
